Codex Pacer 3.2.2 lets you open Codex or Claude straight from the island, and smooths the task badge when both are running.

- **App buttons:** the expanded island's task header now has a Codex button and a Claude button beside **Mark All as Read**, in each service's color. Click one to bring that desktop app forward, even when it has no task running. A service whose desktop app isn't installed gets no button, and you can hide them with **Settings → General → Show App Buttons**.
- When Codex and Claude run at the same time, both arcs around the task badge now have a clean head and stay in their own places, and an arc grows in or fades out smoothly when its service starts or stops.

### Install

- **Existing installation:** use **Check for Updates** to update in place. Your preferences are preserved.
- **Manual installation:** quit Codex Pacer, replace the copy in Applications with the new app, then open it again.
- Requires macOS 14 or later.

See the [user guide](https://github.com/RyanZhangNTU/codex-pacer/blob/main/docs/guide.md) and the [full changelog](https://github.com/RyanZhangNTU/codex-pacer/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
