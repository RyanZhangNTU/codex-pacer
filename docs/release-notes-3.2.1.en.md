Codex Pacer 3.2.1 lets you clear all ended tasks with one click, and fixes Claude tasks that stayed running and Codex questions that could go without a reminder.

- **Mark All as Read:** the new button next to the pin in the expanded island clears every ended task at once, on all pages. Tasks that are running or waiting for your reply or approval stay in the list, and cleared tasks don't come back after a restart.
- **What's New in Settings:** read the highlights of every update again in Settings → What's New, just above Help.
- Claude Desktop tasks no longer stay marked as running after the reply finishes when Desktop saves its records out of order.
- Codex Desktop questions now get a reminder even when Codex stops sending live updates to Pacer, and the reminder clears once you reply. Approval requests still rely on live updates.

### Install

- **Existing installation:** use **Check for Updates** to update in place. Your preferences are preserved.
- **Manual installation:** quit Codex Pacer, replace the copy in Applications with the new app, then open it again.
- Requires macOS 14 or later.
