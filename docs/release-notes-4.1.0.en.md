Codex Pacer 4.1.0 adds DeepSeek budgets, spend alerts and a price clock.

- **Balance or budget:** switch DeepSeek to daily and weekly budgets in **Settings → DeepSeek**. The dial then paces today and this week like Codex's 5-hour and weekly quota, and DeepSeek can take one of the collapsed quota rings. Pacer only reminds; it never blocks a request.
- **Spend not on this Mac:** Pacer checks balance changes against its own estimate, so web searches, background requests and other devices count toward today's spend and your budgets.
- **One alert at a time:** the collapsed bar shows DeepSeek's most urgent alert: balance used up or low, a spend spike in the last 10 minutes, or a budget used up or at 80%. Set the lines in **Settings → Reminders**, or leave them empty for automatic ones.
- **Price clock:** with DeepSeek alone, a 24-hour clock in the collapsed bar shows the peak hours, what today cost and the time now. Choose which module each quota ring shows in **Settings → Collapsed Bar**.
- **A clearer DeepSeek panel:** Today, Speed, where the money went and the last 7 days, each shown only when it has data. Choose Detailed or Simple in **Settings → DeepSeek**.
- **Fixes:** a sharper collapsed quota number, a collapsed bar that keeps its width after a setting changes, and more accurate peak pricing with China's 2026 public holidays.

### Install

- **Existing installation:** use **Check for Updates** to update in place. Your preferences are preserved, and a short What's New note appears once.
- **Manual installation:** quit Codex Pacer, replace the copy in Applications with the new app, then open it again.
- Requires macOS 14 or later. DeepSeek tracking needs DeepSeek Harness on this Mac.
