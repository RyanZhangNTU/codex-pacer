On Windows, Codex tasks run outside the Codex app, such as in a terminal, update right away again instead of up to two minutes late. On Mac and Windows, a Codex task that writes a very long output before it ends now shows as completed instead of "Status unconfirmed".

The Retry and Settings buttons under a quota ring are no longer cut off when three services are shown side by side.
