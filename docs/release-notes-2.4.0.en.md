You can now choose what appears in the collapsed island in Settings, then drag components to arrange their order and left/right placement. Pair your layout with adaptive width to keep the island compact. Especially useful when menu-bar space is limited.

For macOS 14 or later, with support for Apple Silicon and Intel.

For manual installation, quit the old app and drag Codex Pacer from the DMG into Applications. If the unsigned release is blocked, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Security → Open Anyway, then reopen the installer or app.
