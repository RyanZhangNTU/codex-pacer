Codex Pacer 3.2.0 lets you place the island yourself, including as a slim strip at a corner of the screen for Macs without a notch.

- Settings → Appearance now offers **Notch** and **Custom**. With Custom, choose a **Screen Position**: centered below the menu bar, or docked as a narrow vertical strip against the side of the screen in any corner.
- The docked strip stacks the collapsed components, and the panel opens beside it.
- Claude Desktop tasks no longer stay marked as running when the next-step box ends a turn. They show as finished, and Pacer reminds you to reply.

### Install

- **Existing installation:** use **Check for Updates** to update in place. Your preferences are preserved.
- **Manual installation:** quit Codex Pacer, replace the copy in Applications with the new app, then open it again.
- Requires macOS 14 or later.

See the [user guide](https://github.com/RyanZhangNTU/codex-pacer/blob/main/docs/guide.md) and the [full changelog](https://github.com/RyanZhangNTU/codex-pacer/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
