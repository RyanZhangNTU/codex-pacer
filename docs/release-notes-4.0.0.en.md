Codex Pacer 4.0.0 adds DeepSeek Harness, with a balance instead of quota windows.

- **DeepSeek Harness tasks:** Harness sessions on this Mac join the task list beside Codex and Claude Code, with their stage, speed, turn time, model and reasoning effort, approvals and questions that wait for you, and completion cards and notifications. Clicking one brings Harness forward.
- **Turn cost:** each DeepSeek task shows its turn's estimated cost, such as **≈¥0.42**, from its tokens and DeepSeek's published prices, doubled at peak times. Your platform bill is authoritative.
- **Day dial:** DeepSeek's ring is today on a 24-hour face, showing what each hour cost, the doubled-price peak hours and your balance in the middle. With DeepSeek alone, the panel adds how long the balance lasts and the last week's spend split by price.
- **Balance, your way:** sign in to the DeepSeek platform once in Pacer's own window, or paste an API key, whichever matches how Harness signs in. Pacer never reads Harness's own credentials.
- **Low balance:** an outlined **¥** warning on the collapsed bar, and a notification if you want one, when the balance drops below your platform alert line or would run out within three days. An optional **×2** marks peak price.

### Install

- **Existing installation:** use **Check for Updates** to update in place. Your preferences are preserved, and the welcome guide opens once to introduce the DeepSeek module.
- **Manual installation:** quit Codex Pacer, replace the copy in Applications with the new app, then open it again.
- Requires macOS 14 or later. DeepSeek tracking needs DeepSeek Harness on this Mac.
