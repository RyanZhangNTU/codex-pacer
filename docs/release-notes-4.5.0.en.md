**WorkBuddy and WorkBuddy AI.** Pacer follows WorkBuddy and its international edition, WorkBuddy AI, as it does Codex and Claude: their tasks, the progress ring and your account credits, with a reminder before credits run low or expire. Sign in once in Settings; a small guide shows you each click to turn the ring on.

**Module Library.** The Settings sidebar lists only the services on this computer. The Module Library lists every service, where you can turn on or get the others.

**Codex progress on SSH hosts.** Codex tasks on SSH hosts, and in WSL on Windows, show the progress ring too. Tasks already running start reporting once Codex reconnects.

**When updates can't reach GitHub.** If Pacer can't check for or download an update, it tells you, and offers three ways to get the full installer: the GitHub Release, pacer.bhgmz.com, which needs no GitHub, and the QQ group.

**Fixes.** After sleep or a VPN reconnect, SSH tasks come back as soon as the network does, without restarting Pacer. Progress rings move more steadily, a Codex chat outside a project shows its own name while it runs, and on a bar docked to the side, quota and balances are as easy to read as the speed above them. On Windows, sign-in windows close by themselves once your account is confirmed, task updates that arrive in a burst are no longer missed, DeepSeek Harness 0.2.2 opens again from the panel and from its tasks, and What's New for a new version opens again.
