**Pacer now follows Google Antigravity.** See what each Antigravity task is doing, when it needs your approval, its subagents, its speed and both quota pools, with the same progress ring as Codex and Claude.

Subagents now show under their task, and a finished one no longer counts as running. The island can also open with a click instead of on hover, and each SSH host can be monitored or not on its own.

Stability improvements and bug fixes.
