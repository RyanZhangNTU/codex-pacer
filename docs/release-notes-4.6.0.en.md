**Codex banked resets.** History shows how many resets you have and how long each one has left, and a capsule under Codex's quota in the expanded panel shows the next expiry at a glance. Pacer reminds you before a reset expires; choose how early in **Settings → Notifications** ([#238](https://github.com/RyanZhangNTU/codex-pacer/issues/238)).

**Return to Top Center.** If you often move the island, the expanded panel's header can hold a button that puts it back at the top center. Turn it on under **Expanded Panel → Buttons**.

**Claude app access requests.** When Claude Desktop asks to use an app on your computer, Pacer shows that the task is waiting for you instead of still running.

**Fixes.** After you edit an earlier message in a Codex chat, its task and progress ring keep showing, and on Windows the task no longer disappears ([#259](https://github.com/RyanZhangNTU/codex-pacer/issues/259)). A long Codex task keeps its progress ring to the end. The collapsed bar's status shows when a task waits for background work. On Windows, the collapsed bar's status words are centered again ([#258](https://github.com/RyanZhangNTU/codex-pacer/issues/258)), and dates and times follow your time zone instead of GMT ([#264](https://github.com/RyanZhangNTU/codex-pacer/issues/264)).
